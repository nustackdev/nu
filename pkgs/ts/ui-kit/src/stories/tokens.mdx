# Tokens

Three layers. L1 is the nustack color language, L2 the kit role tokens (both themes), L3 the Tailwind v4 `@theme inline` map. Every primitive reaches only for L2/L3 names. L1 lives in `kit/src/index.css` and nowhere else; L3 in `kit/src/theme.css`.

Source of truth is the Go design space: `design/language/color.md` for every value, `design/systems/kit/palette.md` for which role points at which token. The tables below are read from `index.css` itself, light and dark side by side.

## Grounds

Two themes, one cool lean (hue 264):

- **Paper** (light): white document, cards and menus. Sidebar, page ground, hover ground and lines carry a barely there tint (chroma 0.003).
- **Dusk** (dark): the brand's Dusk Navy at half its chroma. Every step carries the same tint.

## States

Hover and press are one wash each, `bg-hover` and `bg-press`, a few percent of `ink-1` over whatever is below. They share the ground's tint, read on any surface and stack. Never a hue: Afterglow is not a background.

Tree and sidebar rows share one ladder: hover `bg-hover`, open elsewhere half of it, selected `bg-press`.

## Accent as text

The Afterglow fill reads 3.4:1 as text on the dark ground, so text and icons never use `accent`. They use `accent-text`. `bg-accent` stays for fills, with white `accent-fg` on top.

## Text tiers

`text-muted` is labels, timestamps and hints only, never body copy.

## Focus ring

`afterglow-text` in both themes, 2px wide, 2px offset.

## Destructive

Solid destructive buttons fill with `status-danger-solid` and a white label. Outline and ghost destructive buttons and menu items use `status-danger` on `status-danger-wash`.
